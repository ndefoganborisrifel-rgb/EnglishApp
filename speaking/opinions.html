Exprimer son opinion

Give your opinion, agree and disagree politely. Essential phrases for any conversation.

📘 La règle

Donner son avis (to give an opinion) : I think (that)… / In my opinion,… / I believe…
I think it's a good idea. In my opinion, we should try.

Approuver (to agree) : I agree. / That's right. / Exactly! / I think so too.

Contredire (to disagree) : I disagree. / I'm not sure about that. / Actually, I think… / I don't really agree because…

Justifier (to give a reason) : because + proposition, so + conséquence.
I like it because it's easy. It was hot, so we went swimming.

💡 "I think" + structure de phrase

"I think" est suivi de that (facultatif) + sujet + verbe. Ne dites jamais "I think it is a good, the idea" — gardez une structure claire : I think (that) + phrase complète.

🔤 Tableau des phrases clés

Fonction Anglais Français
Donner son avis I think (that) it's interesting. Je pense que c'est intéressant.
Mon opinion In my opinion, we need more time. À mon avis, nous avons besoin de plus de temps.
Approuver I agree with you. Je suis d'accord avec vous.
Totalement d'accord Exactly! / That's right! Exactement ! / C'est exact !
Désaccord poli I'm not sure about that. Je ne suis pas vraiment sûr(e) de ça.
Désaccord direct I disagree. I think… Je ne suis pas d'accord. Je pense…
Justifier …because it helps everyone. …parce que ça aide tout le monde.
Conséquence …so it's the best option. …donc c'est la meilleure option.
Hésiter Well… / Hmm, let me think… Eh bien… / Hmm, laisse-moi réfléchir…
Donner un exemple For example, / For instance, Par exemple,

💬 Dialogue modèle

🗣️ Dialogue modèle

Topic : Should students use phones in class?

— What do you think about phones in class?

— Qu'est-ce que tu penses des téléphones en classe ?

— In my opinion, phones are a big distraction. Students don't focus on the lesson.

— À mon avis, les téléphones sont une grande distraction. Les élèves ne se concentrent pas sur le cours.

— I see your point. But I disagree a little. I think phones can be useful — for research, for example.

— That's true. But I believe students use them mostly for social media, not for studying.

— Hmm, I'm not sure about that. It depends on the student, I think.

— Maybe you're right. What do you think the solution is?

— Well, perhaps teachers could allow phones for specific activities, but not all the time.

— I agree with that. It's a fair compromise.

⚠️ Erreurs fréquentes

À éviter

  • I think it's a good, the idea. → I think it's a good idea. (pas d'article redondant)
  • I am agree. → I agree. (agree est un verbe, pas un adjectif ; pas de "am")
  • I think, this is wrong. → I think this is wrong. (pas de virgule après "I think" en milieu de phrase)
  • I don't agree with his opinion about it. → I disagree with his opinion. / I don't agree with him. (éviter les lourdeurs)
  • Because I am tired, so I left. → Because I was tired, I left. OU I was tired, so I left. (because et so ne s'utilisent pas ensemble dans la même phrase)

👨‍🏫 Exercices guidés

À faire ensemble, à l'oral. La réponse est donnée pour servir de modèle.

  • Traduisez : « Je pense que c'est une bonne idée. » → I think it's a good idea.
  • Approuvez poliment : votre interlocuteur dit "I love this film." Vous êtes d'accord. → I agree! / I think so too! / Exactly!
  • Désaccord poli : votre interlocuteur dit "Football is boring." → I don't agree / I disagree. I think it's exciting.
