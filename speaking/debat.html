Débattre et argumenter

Structure oral arguments, present counterarguments and reach a position confidently.

📘 La règle

Structuring an argument (structurer un argument) requires a claim, supporting evidence, and a conclusion : The main issue is… / This is because… / As a result,…

Conceding and countering (concéder et contrer) : While I understand that…, I still think… / That may be true, but… / I take your point, however…

Introducing counterarguments (introduire un contre-argument) : On the one hand… on the other hand… / Some people argue that…, but…

Reaching a conclusion : Taking everything into account, … / On balance, … / All things considered,…

💡 Vary your discourse markers (marqueurs discursifs)

At B2, vary your discourse markers to avoid repetition. Don't start every sentence with "I think" — use I would argue, It seems to me that, One could say that…

💬 Dialogue modèle

🗣️ Dialogue modèle — Débat

Topic : "Should social media be regulated by governments?"

— I would argue that social media should be regulated more strictly. The main reason is that harmful content spreads too easily and affects vulnerable people.

— I take your point, but I'm concerned about freedom of speech. Regulation could be misused to silence political opponents.

— That's a valid concern. However, we already regulate television and newspapers. Why should social media be different?

— Some might argue that the internet is a unique space for free expression. On the other hand, I do agree that there needs to be some form of accountability.

— Exactly. In fact, most democracies already have some online regulations — the question is where to draw the line.

— On balance, I think targeted regulation — against hate speech and misinformation — is reasonable, as long as it's transparent.

— I agree. All things considered, the key is to regulate content, not opinions.

⚠️ Erreurs fréquentes

À éviter

  • Avoid repeating "I think" → vary with I would argue / It seems to me / I believe / One could say
  • Despite of the problem, → Despite the problem, … (Despite ≠ Despite of — pas de "of")
  • Although it's true, but I disagree. → Although it's true, I disagree. (Although et but ne se combinent pas)
  • On the contrary, I think the same. → In contrast / On the other hand (on the contrary = l'opposé total, pas une nuance)
  • Confusing argument (raisonnement, thèse) et argument en français (dispute, querelle).

👨‍🏫 Exercices guidés

À faire ensemble, à l'oral. La réponse est donnée pour servir de modèle.

  • Introduce a counterargument: "Some students think homework is useful." Counter it politely. → "I take that point, however, research suggests that too much homework can increase stress."
  • Concede + counter: "Agree that cars pollute, but argue they are still necessary." → "While it's true that cars are a major source of pollution, they remain essential in areas with poor public transport."
  • Conclude: Sum up a debate about renewable energy. → "On balance, transitioning to renewable energy is both necessary and economically viable in the long term."
