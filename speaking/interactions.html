Interactions sociales

Handle everyday social situations in English: phone calls, invitations, apologies, shopping and small talk.

📘 La règle

Phone calls (appels téléphoniques) : Hello, this is [name] speaking. / Could I speak to…, please? / I'll call back later. / Can I take a message?

Invitations — Accepter : That sounds great! / I'd love to. / Count me in! — Refuser poliment : I'm afraid I can't. / I'm busy that day, but maybe another time? / Thanks for the invitation.

Apologies (excuses) : I'm sorry. / I apologise. / I'm really sorry about that. / It won't happen again. — Accepter : That's OK. / No worries. / Don't worry about it.

Shopping (faire des courses / du shopping) : Can I help you? / I'm just looking, thanks. / Do you have this in a larger size? / How much is this? / I'll take it.

💡 Register matters (le registre est important)

Au téléphone avec un inconnu → formel : Could I speak to…?
Avec un ami → informel : Is Tom there?

💬 Dialogue modèle

🗣️ Dialogue — Au téléphone puis au magasin

Dialogue A — Appel téléphonique :

— Hello, this is Sophie speaking. Could I speak to David, please?

— Bonjour, c'est Sophie à l'appareil. Puis-je parler à David, s'il vous plaît ?

— I'm afraid David is not available at the moment. Can I take a message?

— David n'est pas disponible pour le moment. Puis-je prendre un message ?

— Yes, please. Could you tell him that Sophie called and ask him to call me back?

— Of course. I'll pass that on.

— Thank you very much. Goodbye!

— Goodbye!

Dialogue B — En magasin :

— Can I help you?

— Puis-je vous aider ?

— Yes, please. I'm looking for a jacket. Do you have this one in black?

— Oui, merci. Je cherche une veste. Vous avez celle-ci en noir ?

— Let me check… I'm afraid we only have it in grey or navy blue.

— I'll try the navy blue one. Do you have it in a medium?

— Yes, here you go. The fitting rooms are on your left.

— Thank you. How much is it?

— It's £45.

— Great, I'll take it!

⚠️ Erreurs fréquentes

À éviter

  • I am Sophie speaking. → This is Sophie speaking. / It's Sophie. (pour se présenter au téléphone)
  • I want to speak David. → Could I speak to David? (préposition to obligatoire)
  • I am sorry for that. → I'm sorry about that. / I apologise for that.
  • How much it costs? → How much is it? / How much does it cost? (ordre sujet-verbe en question)
  • I'm just looking for. → I'm just looking, thank you. (looking for seulement si suivi d'un objet)

👨‍🏫 Exercices guidés

À faire ensemble, à l'oral. La réponse est donnée pour servir de modèle.

  • Vous appelez un hôtel : dites que vous souhaitez parler au directeur. → "Could I speak to the manager, please?"
  • Votre ami vous invite mais vous ne pouvez pas venir : excusez-vous et proposez une autre fois. → "I'm afraid I can't make it, but maybe another time?"
  • En magasin : demandez si la veste est disponible en rouge et en taille L. → "Do you have this jacket in red? In a size L?"
