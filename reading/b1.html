Lecture B1 — Working from home

Un article d'opinion sur le télétravail. Lisez le texte, apprenez le vocabulaire, puis répondez aux questions de compréhension.

📖 Texte

Ten years ago, almost everybody travelled to an office every morning. Today, millions of people work from home, and many of them say they will never go back to the old way.

There are clear advantages. Workers save time and money because they do not commute. They can start earlier, take a short break for lunch, and spend more time with their families. Some companies have also noticed that employees are more productive when they are not interrupted by colleagues all day. For people who live far from big cities, remote work opens doors that were closed before.

However, working from home is not perfect. Many employees feel lonely and miss the social contact of the office. It can be difficult to separate work and private life, and some people end up working longer hours than before. Younger workers, in particular, often learn faster when they sit next to experienced colleagues.

In my opinion, the best solution is a hybrid model: two or three days at home and the rest in the office. This way, we keep the freedom of remote work without losing the human side of a team. The future of work is probably not fully remote, but it will certainly never be the same again.
