Lecture A1 — My family

Un texte court et simple au présent. Lisez-le, apprenez le vocabulaire, puis répondez aux questions de compréhension.

📖 Texte

Hello! My name is Lucy. I am ten years old. I live in a small house with my family. We are five people.

My father is called John. He is a teacher. My mother is called Mary. She is a nurse. I have one brother and one sister. My brother is Tom. He is twelve. My sister is Emma. She is six.

We have a dog. His name is Max. He is black and white. We love Max very much. Every morning, my mother makes breakfast and we eat together. I am very happy with my family.
