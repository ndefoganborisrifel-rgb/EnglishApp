Lecture A2 — A day in London

Un récit au passé. Lisez l'histoire d'une journée à Londres, apprenez le vocabulaire, puis répondez aux questions.

📖 Texte

Last Saturday, Anna and her friend Paul visited London for the day. They took an early train from Brighton and arrived at Victoria Station at nine o'clock in the morning.

First, they walked to Buckingham Palace and watched the guards. Then they took a red bus to the British Museum. Anna loved the Egyptian rooms, but Paul preferred the Greek statues. At one o'clock, they were hungry, so they ate fish and chips in a small café near the river.

In the afternoon, they went on the London Eye. From the top, they saw the whole city. It started to rain, so they bought two umbrellas. In the evening, they were very tired but very happy. They took the last train home and talked about their wonderful day.
